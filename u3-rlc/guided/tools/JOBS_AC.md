## Style (append to every job)
Style: vintage technical illustration in the manner of a 1950s electronics handbook, confident navy-blue ink
line work with fine cross-hatching, soft watercolor washes in teal, amber and coral, on clean off-white paper.
No text, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no writing of any kind, no people, no faces, no logos.
